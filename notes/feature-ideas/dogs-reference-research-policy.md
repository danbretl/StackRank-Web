# Dogs reference research and production

Version: `dogs-reference-research-2026-10-03.1`.

Dan approved this policy on October 3, 2026 and requested continued production with more efficient
batching, without degrading quality. It supersedes the Commons-only original-photo prerequisite
in older Dogs kickoff/handoff instructions. The earlier rolling-queue authorization also remains
in force. At authorization, cohort L had14 published additions; it subsequently completed the same100-addition target. Further runs require fresh authorization.

## Research and generator inputs

Use reputable breed organizations, registries, standards, original studies and credible identified
adult photographs to establish the exact breed's factual appearance. Publicly viewable photos may
inform factual research; public availability does not make them licensed generator inputs.
Research can combine complementary views and a standard's anatomical description. Do not require
one photograph to show every feature when the combined evidence establishes it accurately.
Uncertain identity, ambiguous crosses, juvenile-only evidence and historical reconstruction remain
unqualified. Parent breeds do not establish a cross's character or exact appearance.

Prefer a **text-only generation prompt** when independently written, evidence-backed morphology
is sufficient. Omit image reference arguments entirely. Create a new pose, composition, setting
and lighting; do not describe a source photograph's distinctive arrangement for recreation.
Record the exact research URLs, source roles, actual paragraphs/visual observations and acquisition
disclosures. Preserve supporting text and audit evidence; do not collect unlicensed photo originals
merely to fill an old archive field. Do not label research-only material as a licensed photo input.

When a photograph is supplied directly to the image generator, verify and retain its original
bytes, actual creator/source chain and license or permission covering the intended use. Commons
is one eligible host; creators, Flickr, museums and breed organizations can also host qualifying
originals. Follow the actual license conditions. A public page, uploader label or license badge
alone does not settle a contradictory source chain. No paid acquisition, external correspondence
or separately billed generation API is authorized by this amendment.

For every portrait, root reads the actual supporting evidence, reviews the exact prompt and
approves the selected input mode. Licensed-input mode additionally requires whole-input visual
and rights review. Text-only mode requires explicit empty image inputs and a bound morphology
research dossier. All native outputs still receive individual identity, anatomy, full-body/paw/tail,
composition and close-reproduction review, plus a whole contact-sheet review. The established
1536×1024 landscape and product quality requirements are unchanged.

Photograph display, public-snapshot artwork and raster-export purposes remain denied. Research
images are not published. Earlier source-specific holds remain historical facts; a new text-only
route may use newly adjudicated factual evidence without pretending the rejected photo or grant
has become approved. Never infer unavailable anatomical facts from a failed reference.

## Efficient rolling batches

Reuse the same three workers. Root assigns disjoint packets of approximately 3–5 identities,
favoring already reviewed copy and strong exact-breed standards. Within an assigned packet,
workers may perform bounded relevant research without a separate root permission for each URL.
Record sources once and reuse exact retained primary and peer evidence when unchanged. Avoid
rebuilding broad inventories, repeating failed chains or speculative license rescue. Commons
requests, if useful, still use the shared 5.1-second gate and Retry-After handling.

Batch source acquisition, morphology preparation and independent editorial review. Root may
review several complete packets together, but records an individual decision and immutable
approval for every image call. Keep up to three worker image calls running, one per worker,
using existing atomic permits. Each distinct portrait still gets one built-in tool call.
Appending a tranche preserves unstarted approvals from earlier tranches: the coordinator
rechecks their original immutable freeze, identity, qualification and approval hashes.

Publish a stable completed batch around 10–20 pairs when available; release smaller batches when
that prevents a slow candidate from holding ready work. Prepare the next small packet while
generation or release checks run. Perform the full verification suite once per stable release
candidate, not per dog. Retain exact source/text/prompt/input/native/peer/root/archive bindings
and verify every new live pair. Root remains the integration and main-release owner.

This is a workflow policy, not a blanket legal conclusion that all inspiration is exempt from
copyright. General references: [photographic expression](https://www.copyright.gov/engage/photographers/),
[facts and ideas versus expression](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html),
[case-specific fair use](https://www.copyright.gov/fair-use/), and
[CC BY 4.0 conditions](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
